## Fix and safeguards

The adapter response waiter and Driver heartbeat share a task/document/conversation-scoped observation. Normal count growth retains its fence. For windowed live responses, pre-Send nodes/text are excluded and observed append growth on the same eligible node while generating establishes the response. Restored sent waits exclude older visible turns and observe current-turn growth. Text stays in memory; only numeric length reaches Decision, whose ten-minute no-progress policy and monotonic high-water mark are unchanged.

Strictly validated exact-task/revision/Review-target results can confirm Send during recovery. Driver passes the authoritative task and then uses normal fenced extraction and receipt submission; it never clicks Send again in that path.

Regression coverage includes live and reconstructed adapters growing beyond ten minutes, stopped growth, edited old messages, static rerender, route drift, invalid task/revision/HEAD/schema proofs, and an actual HTTP Bridge `prompt_submitting` recovery that accepts the result with zero Send clicks. Existing multi-turn and pre-Send-result fences also pass.
